01 / ABOUT

Engineering decisions backed by data.

My research sits at the intersection of geotechnical earthquake engineering, natural-hazard assessment, and machine learning. I build computational workflows that turn complex seismic, rainfall, topographic, geological, and geospatial data into practical hazard information.

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Old Dominion University, where my work includes automated usability classification of ground-motion recordings, probabilistic rainfall-triggered landslide hazard assessment, and site characterization using ambient vibration measurements.
